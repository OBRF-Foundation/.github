## 🌿 Who We Are

The **Olive Branch Revival Foundation (OBRF)** is a Canadian CRA-registered non-profit charity dedicated to nurturing a culture where all people can **access, participate, contribute, and thrive** in meaningful and dignified ways.

We serve a diverse audience originating from **60+ nations and ethnicities** — passionate people working hard to call our community home. On average, newcomers face up to 10 years of integration challenges. OBRF exists to shorten that journey.

---

## 🎯 Vision & Mission

**Vision**
> OBRF envisions a community where everyone feels a profound sense of belonging.

**Mission**
> OBRF supports newcomers, marginalized individuals, and diverse communities by providing essential services, fostering cultural and interfaith understanding, and advancing equitable access to opportunity. Through collaborative programs, education, and systems change, we empower people to build dignified, self-sufficient lives rooted in belonging and mutual care.

---

## 🤝 How We Work

We believe lasting impact requires intentional collaboration. OBRF acts as a **bridge** — connecting individuals, organizations, and communities through trust-based partnerships that increase integration and belonging. When partners are involved early, projects become stronger, more sustainable, and truly community-owned.

## 📦 Our Programs

| Program | Description |
|---|---|
| 🪑 **Furniture & Clothes** | Pickup and delivery of donated furniture and clothing to families in need |
| 🥫 **Mobile Food Bank** | Bringing food support directly to communities |
| 🌍 **Cultural Sensitivity Training** | Workshops that build cross-cultural understanding and empathy |
| 💻 **Technology Access** | Bridging the digital divide with devices and connectivity support |
| ⚽ **Sports** | Using sport as a vehicle for inclusion and community building |
| 🎓 **Education & Conferences** | Learning events that elevate voices and share knowledge across cultures |
